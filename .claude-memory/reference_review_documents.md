## Rules the documents are held to

- **No meta-narrative in the presentable one.** "An earlier version proposed…",
  "corrected 2026-09-…" belong in the record. This was violated and stripped.
- **The relational map's bold means one thing**: a value held by at most 3 of the 18
  rows. Declared in the caption. No row name is emphasised — bolding MapFormer's row
  in a review by its author reads as advocacy, and did.
- **MapPoPE is marked as this work's construction**, not published. Every other row
  is published; a reader takes an 18-row map of the literature literally.
- The relational map is **Table 4** in the review, not Table 1.
