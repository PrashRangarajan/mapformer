## 4. What is prior art (from `docs/lit/`)

Context-gated, window and hidden-state step generators (Mamba-1/2/3, Selective RoPE, PaTH, RWKV-7, CoPE) and
identity-at-init (ReZero, Flamingo, Mamba's dt bias) are published; HSR is that trick. "Separation of what and
where is learned" is in MapFormer's own Fig. 9 and in trained-LM studies; TEM / TEM-t / CSCG / Vector-HaSH
architect the split with action-only updates (ActOnly is TEM-t's update). Transfer to unseen iid codes through a
shared linear encoder is Chen et al. 2019. Possibly ours: cue distance x generator source on a signed path phase;
the causal score form in path models; separation without map redraw; a measured content-to-step leak and its
in-distribution cost.
