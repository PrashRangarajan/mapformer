rarely learn one). Draft 4 is ~5,900 words including references (~8 pages of main text by estimate): it fits 8-9 page
tracks as is; a 4-page track needs a cut to Secs. 4.1-4.3 plus Lemma 1.

stated.

## Top 3 (recommendation)

1. **An ICLR 2027 workshop** (San Francisco per the workshop call; workshops 29-30 Apr 2027). Workshop list due
   **29 Nov 2026** (VERIFIED); paper deadlines are set per workshop, ICLR's suggested date is **1 Feb 2027**
   (VERIFIED as a suggestion; each workshop's own date UNVERIFIED until its call is out). Best-fitting candidates if
   they recur (UNVERIFIED that they do): Sci4DL (2nd ed. ICLR 2026; 4 pages, double-blind, non-archival, explicitly
   for falsifying hypotheses with controlled experiments), ICBINB (negative results; ran at ICLR 2025 and 2026), NFAM
   (associative memory; ICLR 2025, 2026). ICLR asks every 2027 workshop to run a tiny/short-paper track. Highest
   expected fit and audience; non-archival, so TMLR stays open.
2. **CCN 2027** (Cognitive Computational Neuroscience; Edinburgh, 26-29 Jul 2027). 8-page proceedings track:
   abstract **1 Feb 2027**, full paper **4 Feb 2027** (VERIFIED); 2-page extended abstract **1 Apr 2027** (VERIFIED).
   The one venue where the cognitive-map / path-integration / TEM framing is the core audience. Proceedings papers got
   an OpenReview DOI in 2026 (2027 archival terms UNVERIFIED); rejected 8-pagers were rerouted to the 2-page track in
   2026. The 2-page track is a cheap fallback that collides with nothing.
3. **CPAL 2027** (Conference on Parsimony and Learning; Tokyo, 23-26 Mar 2027). **Recent Spotlight** track:
   **18 Jan 2027** (VERIFIED), non-archival, single-blind, under-review papers allowed, 250-word abstract + manuscript.
   Proceedings track (archival, PMLR, 9 pages, double-blind): abstract **13 Nov 2026**, paper **25 Nov 2026**
   (VERIFIED). Fit medium: parameterisation / low-rank step map can be framed as parsimony, but the paper's centre is
   learnability, not parsimony. The Spotlight track is the useful part: it can carry the paper alongside an ICLR
   workshop submission.

Recommendation: target (1) as the primary, with the full 8-page version cut to the workshop's limit once the list
appears on 29 Nov. Sci4DL-style calls say work accepted at a prior workshop must add new results, so do NOT take the
paper to a non-archival workshop that accepts before ICLR's decisions unless that is acceptable; the CPAL Spotlight
(non-archival, explicitly allows concurrent submissions) and CCN's 2-page track are the safe parallel options. If
feedback before February matters more than the venue, AAAI-27 MATH4AI (20 Nov 2026, non-archival) is the earliest
open slot, but its fit is weak. TMLR afterwards for the archival version.
