# Audit, 2026-09-10: the EM/WM kernel-theory chain

Two independent agents went through everything produced 2026-09-09..10: one
re-derived every number from checkpoints and raw JSON; one refereed the maths,
pre-registration compliance, design confounds and code. **Every finding below was
re-verified against the code or data before being recorded here; one was
overruled.** This file supersedes the corresponding claims in `THEORY_KERNEL.md`,
`TALE_OF_TWO_ALGORITHMS.md`, `RECENCY_EM_RESULTS.md`, `N5_RESULTS.md`,
`DOF_RESULTS.md` and `D5_RESULTS.md`, each of which now carries a pointer here.

## Findings that change a conclusion

**1. MapWM is not additive.** (`model.py:224-232`, verified.) `WMTransformerLayer`
rotates the *content-derived* `Q` and `K` by the path angle, so the WM score is

    Q_t^T R(theta_s - theta_t) K_s  =  sum_b |Q_b||K_b| cos(omega_b dS + phi_b(q, k))

-- a position kernel whose amplitudes AND phases are set by the query and key
content. EM's is `A_X (*) kappa(dS)` with `kappa` content-independent. The
"AND-gate vs OR-gate" framing (CLAUDE.md since 2026-05-10), Thm 3's `A_X + kappa`
and its gradient identity `d score / d A_X = 1`, and the "sum" in
`TALE_OF_TWO_ALGORITHMS.md` are all wrong about MapWM. **The real contrast: EM's
position kernel is shared by every query-key pair; WM's has per-pair amplitudes
and phases.**

**2. Recency does not require a clock, and EM can represent it exactly.**
Construction (independent of the auditor's): symbols `Delta = (1,0)`, `q_k`
`Delta = (-(k-1), 0)`, MASK `(0,1)`, filler 0. The inclusive `cumsum`
(`model.py:110`) makes the query token rewind the count by `k-1`, so the retrieval
offset is **0 for every query**; `min_gap = k_max` keeps earlier queries out of the
window and the MASK axis separates older cycles. A **single-`p_0` kernel (rho = 1,
zero phase freedom)** with the real `omega` schedule and random magnitudes and
projections scores **1423/1423, 1417/1417, 1415/1415**. The same kernel without the
rewind scores **0.0857** (most-recent floor 0.077). Consequences:

- Thm 3's corollary ("EM << WM when `delta` varies across queries") rests on a
  false premise: **`delta` is chosen by the model, not fixed by the task.**
- EM's -0.375 on recency is a **learnability** failure, not a limit of the class.
- Thm 1's "a clock needs `S` strictly monotone" does not apply to recency. The
  crossover's recency half showed a monotone increment is *harmless* there
  (-0.004), not that recency *needs* one.

**3. The observation the q0/k0 line set out to explain does not replicate on fresh
seeds.** `sep - P0` on recency: seeds 0-7 **+0.237** (8/8); seeds 8-23 alone
**+0.073** (9/16, MDE 0.130, unmeasured). The pooled n=24 +0.128 is carried by the
original eight. **Phase freedom does replicate**: `AlignFree - AlignLock` on seeds
8-23 alone is **+0.173** (13/16, MDE 0.127). The mechanism is now better supported
than the phenomenon it was introduced to explain.

**4. N5's `|rho|` effect is established only for a near-zero-amplitude kernel.**
Peak `sum a_i / sqrt(d)`: every frozen N5 arm **0.003**; trainable arms learn
**0.27-0.50** on recency (`||q0||` 0.15 -> 1.46-1.96) and **0.06-0.08** on the
torus. "Freezing is catastrophic on recency" is confounded with amplitude, and the
+0.292 is a statement about a frozen kernel ~100x smaller than a learned one.

**5. N5's registered P1 and P2 had expectation exactly zero before any run.**
`(W_q, b_q) -> (-W_q, -b_q)` maps the `minus` arm onto the `plus` arm exactly, and
the init is sign-symmetric. The `|rho|` axis was chosen after the data (labelled
"restated", not registered). The line "3 of 8 seeds start with every head at
`rho < 0` -- the kernel actively down-weights the same location" contradicts the
same gauge argument and is **withdrawn**.

**6. D3 compares two kinds of measurement.** Its recency term is accuracy at the
TRAINING length (T=1024), where `r(loss, acc) = -0.985`; its torus term is
out-of-distribution accuracy at 8x training length and is unmeasured on its own
(-0.088, MDE 0.118). D3 clears its MDE only by subtracting an unmeasured term of
opposite sign. What it reproduces is the `sep`-vs-`P0` sign flip, not the
mechanism. With the n=24 recency term it is +0.253.

**7. Rule-9 loss-matching conditions on a mediator.** A function-class limit also
shows up as worse fit, so a zero loss-matched residual cannot by itself distinguish
optimisation from representation. The "optimisation" label on the recency effects
is right -- but because of finding 2 (the locked arms CAN represent the task), not
because of rule 9.

**8. AlignFree and AlignLock differ in optimiser dynamics, not only phase freedom.**
Under Adam, `k0` entries (~0.02) move ~5% of their size per step; the scale `s`
(~1.0) moves ~0.1%. Weight decay alone predicts `s -> 0.674` (recency) and
`0.447` (torus); measured medians are ~0.70 and ~0.51. So "magnitude freedom buys
nothing" is closer to "a parameter the optimiser barely moved buys nothing" -- and
since the claim is about optimisation, the confound sits on the claimed axis. The
control rejected in `DOF_PREREG.md` (a free per-block 2-vector with its angle
discarded) matches the optimiser treatment and is the one to run.

**9. The registered primary readout of `REC_EM_PREREG.md` was never reported.**
Computed now -- epochs to training loss below threshold, censored at 300:

| threshold | `Vanilla_r4` (WM) | `VanillaEM_P0_r4` | `VanillaEM_r4` |
|---|---|---|---|
| < 0.5 | 8/8, median epoch 70.5 | **0/8** | 1/8 (epoch 232) |
| < 0.1 | 7/8, median epoch 118 | **0/8** | **0/8** |

P1 is confirmed on the registered readout. P2/P3 were registered at T=2048 and
reported at T=1024; at T=2048 they are -0.437 and -0.218, verdicts unchanged.

**10. D4's "exact reproduction" was the same computation twice.** The `sep` and
`P0` checkpoints for seeds 0-7 in `runs/dof/recency` and `runs/recency_em` are
**bitwise identical, 16/16**, weights and loss curves. That is determinism, not
replication. `D5_PREREG.md` cited it as the licence to pool; that licence was void
(pooling itself is fine: identical code, all four arms extended by seed).

## Stale, mislabelled or omitted -- no verdict changes

- `THEORY_KERNEL.md` labelled **+0.123/+0.195** as signed vs MONOTONE. They are
  signed vs an INDEX code (`SIGN_ABLATION.md:69,80`). Signed vs monotone is
  -0.215/-0.280 loss-matched.
- `N5_RESULTS.md` omitted recency `plus - zero` = **+0.191, 7/8, MDE 0.158,
  DETECTABLE**. Its "losses 2.02-2.64, barely left chance" gave the range of arm
  MEANS; per run, one `minus` seed reached loss 0.789 and accuracy 0.776.
- n=8 sizes superseded by n=24: `sep - P0` +0.237 -> **+0.128**; phase +0.148 ->
  **+0.165**; magnitude +0.120 -> **-0.033**; coherence -0.031 -> **-0.004**.
- "Final losses order monotonically in total freedom" is false at n=24:
  `P0` 1.143 < `AlignLock` 1.239.
- `RECENCY_EM_RESULTS.md`: WM loss range is 0.008-**0.380**, not 0.044; "EM does
  not learn the task at all" overstates (`P0` 0.600 against chance 0.0625).
- `TALE_OF_TWO_ALGORITHMS.md` is stale: N-back has been run; EM - WM = -0.375;
  App. A.4's separate-`q0/k0` suspicion is no longer "refuted four times" -- it is
  directionally supported on recency and NOT independently replicated (finding 3).
- Thm 1 holds only for a scalar or fully constrained accumulator. The model's is
  rank-`r` per head, so one subspace can cancel while another counts.
- Coherence sd: `sqrt(sum a^2 / 2) / sum a` = 0.159 is right; the "~ 1/sqrt(2 n_b)"
  beside it (0.125) is off by 4/pi.
- **N2 is withdrawn as ill-posed**: `MapFormerWM_PoPE` is a WM model with no
  `q0`/`k0`, so there is no separate-form lottery to measure.
- The MiniGrid coherence probe ran on `VanillaEM_r4` (gap 0.098), not the r=2 arm
  whose gap is the 0.137 quoted beside it.

## Overruled

The numerical auditor called "rho drifts by 0.25-0.27" WRONG. It is right under
the definition the probe actually printed -- mean per-seed absolute change in
head-averaged `rho`: **0.2495 and 0.2663** exactly. The auditor checked two other
definitions. The wording is still ambiguous (signed shifts are -0.048 and -0.172;
per-head absolute drift 0.33 and 0.65) and now says which quantity it means.

## What survived both audits

Every arm table, contrast, MDE and verdict in RECENCY_EM, N5, the DOF torus half
and D5; frozen `rho` held exactly and `sum a_i` matched across N5 arms;
`AlignLock` scales never negative; the rule-9 correlations; the additive
decomposition; complete seed lists; seed-paired evaluators; no pre-registration
edited after its launch commit; no `--fast-attn` in any run.

## Where the account now stands

- **Robust:** phase freedom on recency -- +0.165 at n=24, +0.173 on fresh seeds
  alone -- as an effect on what training FINDS. `|rho|` matters for a frozen,
  low-amplitude kernel on the torus.
- **Dead:** Thm 3 as a composition theorem (WM is not additive); its corollary
  (`delta` is learned); recency's EM deficit read as a function-class limit; N2.
- **Needs restating:** N1 (fixed-`k` recency) was built to test the dead corollary.
  With a fixed `k` the rewind is a constant, so it now tests learnability of a
  simpler rewind rather than anything about query-dependent offsets.

**A hypothesis, post-hoc and untested, and not written into the theory:** the
recency ordering WM 0.975 > EM-`sep` 0.837 > EM-`P0` 0.600 tracks how much phase
freedom each has -- per query-key PAIR (WM, whose phases are set by content) >
`n_b` global phases (EM-`sep`) > none (EM-`P0`). Every arm can represent the task
(the construction covers the weakest), so they would differ only in how easily
training finds the solution. It is consistent with findings 1-3, rule 9, DOF and
D5, and that is all it is.
