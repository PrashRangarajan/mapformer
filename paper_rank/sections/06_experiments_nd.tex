\section{The rank threshold across dimension}
\label{sec:nd}

\PENDING{gates pass and the environment is built; training is not launched.}

\paragraph{What it tests.} Section~\ref{sec:rank} splits rank into a geometric
threshold at $r=D$ and an optimisation threshold above it, and the two are
separable because they predict effects in \emph{different cells} of a
$D\times r$ table. Nothing so far tests the geometric half: every rank
measurement in this paper is at $D=2$, where the bound saturates and predicts
nothing.

\paragraph{Design.} A $D$-dimensional torus (\texttt{environment\_nd.py}): $2D$
actions $\pm e_i$, allocentric aliased observations, the interleaved stream and
revisit-masked loss of the reference task, and the \emph{directed} walk --- draw
an action, repeat it $k\sim U\{1..10\}$ times --- mirrored exactly, since it is
what sets the revisit statistics all $2$D numbers here rest on. State-space size
is held near-constant so only dimensionality moves: $D=2$ at grid $32$ ($1024$
cells), $D=3$ at grid $10$ ($1000$), $D=5$ at grid $4$ ($1024$). Arms
$r\in\{2,D,D{+}2\}$, $8$ seeds, one batch, the recipe of \S\ref{sec:2d}.

\paragraph{Predictions, separated by mechanism.} Geometry predicts an $r=2$
deficit ordered $D{=}5\gg D{=}3>D{=}2$, tracking the measured separation ratios
($11\times$, $2.0\times$, $1.0\times$), and predicts \emph{nothing} for $r=D$
against $r=D{+}2$. Optimisation predicts a further gap from $r=D$ to $r=D{+}2$ of
roughly the $2$D size ($\approx 0.085$), present at every $D$ and not growing
with it.

\paragraph{Falsifiers.} $r=2$ unimpaired at $D=5$ kills the geometric account.
A flat $r{=}D$ vs $r{=}D{+}2$ contrast at every $D$ makes the optimisation half a
$2$D artifact. An $r=2$ deficit that does not grow with $D$ refutes the packing
bound as the mechanism, since the exponent is the only thing $D$ changes.

\paragraph{Gates.} Run before any GPU time; all three configurations pass
(\texttt{ND\_GATES.md}). An $n$-gram on the action stream alone at orders $1$--$5$
reaches at most $0.536$ against a measured majority-class rate of $0.526$; revisit
rates are $0.23/0.28/0.62$ with $30$--$79$ scored positions per trajectory. The
revisit gate is load-bearing rather than a formality: a simple random walk on
$\Z^{D}$ is transient for $D\ge 3$, so revisits exist here only because the torus
is finite, and a configuration where they collapsed would present an absence of
labels as a rank effect. The cross-$D$ difference in revisit rate is a genuine
confound, and is why the primary contrast is \emph{within} $D$.
