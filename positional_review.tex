\large two factors, one scan, and where the arena's mechanisms sit}
\date{}

\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Attention has no intrinsic notion of order, and every positional mechanism is a
choice about how to put one in. This review organises the current arena --- RoPE,
ALiBi, xPos, PoPE, CoPE, MapFormer, Selective RoPE, CARoPE, GRAPE, FoX, Jordan-RoPE,
LieRE, PaTH, DeltaNet, RWKV-7, Mamba and its successors, MesaNet, Titans, TAPE and
TEM --- around a single classification and eight axes of variation.

The classification is sharp. If the positional map is linear in the query and key,
translation-invariant, and continuous, the family of interval operators is a
one-parameter matrix group $A(d) = \exp(dM)$, and classifying positional encodings
reduces to classifying the generator $M$ up to conjugacy. Diagonalising $M$ gives
exactly two live slots --- a real part that scales and an imaginary part that
rotates --- because $\C$ has exactly two. Defective generators add polynomial
factors and nothing else. Everything that appears to escape this picture escapes by
breaking one of the three assumptions, and it is usually possible to say which.

Writing the two slots as one complex log-polar accumulator makes the whole arena a
choice of where content enters --- instantaneously or accumulated, in the magnitude
or in the phase --- and with what sign and rank. Two of those axes turn out to be
where the differences actually live. We give the algebra, the relational map, and
the measurements that separate the axes on a navigation task where they are
distinguishable.
\end{abstract}

\section{The shared frame}
\label{sec:frame}

Rotary attention transforms the query and key of head $h$ by a block-diagonal
rotation before the inner product. Splitting a head's $\hd$ coordinates into
$\nb = \hd/2$ planes and identifying each plane with $\C$, write
$\tilde q_t^{(c)}, \tilde k_s^{(c)} \in \C$ for the query at position $t$ and key at
position $s$ in plane $c$. The pre-softmax logit is
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:polar}
  a_{ts} \;=\; \frac{1}{\sqrt{\hd}}\sum_{c=1}^{\nb}
    \mu^q_{t,c}\,\mu^k_{s,c}\,
    \cos\!\big(\theta^{(c)}_s - \theta^{(c)}_t + \psi^k_{s,c} - \psi^q_{t,c}\big),
\end{equation}
in polar coordinates $\tilde q = \mu^q e^{i(\psi^q + \theta_t)}$,
$\tilde k = \mu^k e^{i(\psi^k + \theta_s)}$. Every logit is a sum of products
$\mu\cos\varphi$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{A positional mechanism is a choice about one of those two
factors.} RoPE, MapFormer, Selective RoPE, CARoPE and Mamba-3's rotation set the
phase $\varphi$. PoPE, ALiBi, the Forgetting Transformer and every gated linear RNN
set the magnitude $\mu$. xPos, MapPoPE and Mamba-3 set both. That is the whole
design space of \eqref{eq:polar}, and $\S$\ref{sec:classification} explains why it
is not an artefact of the parameterisation.

\section{The classification, and why there are two slots}
\label{sec:classification}

Let $\tilde q_t = F(t)q_t$ and $\tilde k_s = G(s)k_s$, so the logit is
$q_t^\top F(t)^\top G(s) k_s$, and impose three conditions:

\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
\item \textbf{Linearity.} $F$ and $G$ act linearly on $q$ and $k$.
\item \textbf{Translation invariance.} $F(t)^\top G(s)$ depends only on $s-t$.
\item \textbf{Continuity} in the interval.
\end{itemize}

\noindent Normalise so $F(t)^\top G(t) = I$, absorbing the constant into the keys.
Then $A(d) := F(0)^\top G(d)$ satisfies $A(0) = I$ and $A(d)A(e) = A(d+e)$: the
interval operators form a \emph{one-parameter matrix group}, so $A(d) = \exp(dM)$
for a fixed generator $M$, and the classification of positional encodings is the
classification of $M$ up to conjugacy. This argument is due to
Puranik (2026); GRAPE (Zhang et al., ICLR 2026) develops the same framework
independently and uses it to prove exact special cases.

Putting $M$ in Jordan form gives three cases and no others.

\begin{itemize}\itemsep3pt
\item \textbf{Diagonalisable, real eigenvalue $\lambda$.} The plane contributes
  $e^{\lambda d}$: exponential decay for $\lambda<0$, NoPE at $\lambda=0$, and a
  blow-up for $\lambda>0$ that no causal design wants.
\item \textbf{Diagonalisable, conjugate pair.} The real canonical form is
  $e^{\lambda d}\,\mathrm{rot}(\omega d)$ --- \emph{damped RoPE}. This is the
  $\operatorname{Re}/\operatorname{Im}$ split of \eqref{eq:polar}, and
  \textbf{there are exactly two slots because $\C$ has exactly two}. RetNet, xPos
  and Mamba-3 all live here.
\item \textbf{Defective.} Repeated eigenvalues with a nilpotent part give
  polynomial factors $d^r$ times the above. ALiBi is realisable this way after
  augmenting $q$ and $k$; Jordan-RoPE (2026) is the deliberate occupant, coupling a
  complex eigenvalue to a nilpotent in the same block to obtain modes
  $d^r e^{i\omega d}$.
\end{itemize}

\noindent A remark of Puranik's is worth isolating because it is the bridge to
everything content-dependent: a gate with a data-dependent decay should be read as
\emph{learning how far to advance time}, not as changing a rate. That is exactly
the substitution $t \mapsto S_t = \sum_{u\le t}G(x_u)$ of $\S$\ref{sec:unify}.

\section{The phase axis: a family of scans}
\label{sec:phase}

Every content-dependent phase mechanism in the arena maintains a state by
cumulative summation and reads it out linearly:
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:gen}
  s_t \;=\; s_{t-1} + \varphi\big(x_{t-K+1},\dots,x_t\big) \in \R^m,
  \qquad
  \theta_t \;=\; A\,s_t \in \R^{H\nb},
\end{equation}
and they differ in three numbers: the \textbf{state rank} $m$, the form of the
increment $\varphi$, and its \textbf{temporal support} $K$.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lllll}
\toprule
mechanism & $m$ & $\varphi$ & $K$ & readout $A$ \\
\midrule
RoPE           & $1$    & $\equiv 1$ (constant)                   & $1$ & $\omega$ \\
MapFormer      & $r$    & $W_{\mathrm{out}}W_{\mathrm{in}}x$      & $1$ & $\operatorname{diag}(\omega)$ \\
CARoPE         & $H$    & $1/(\softplus(xW)+1) \in (0,1)$         & $1$ & geometric \\
Selective RoPE & $H\nb$ & $\sigma(W_gx)\odot(\kappa * W_\omega x)$ & $4$ & $\tau I$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent RoPE is the $\varphi \equiv 1$ special case: $\theta_t = t\,\omega$ is a
clock that advances by the same amount on every token. Everything else lets the
content set the rate.

\subsection{Nesting}
\label{sec:incl}

RoPE $\subset$ MapFormer $\subset$ Selective RoPE, each inclusion \emph{removing a
constraint} rather than adding a mechanism.

\medskip
\noindent\emph{MapFormer inside Selective RoPE.} Four settings suffice: (i) the
convolution kernel becomes a delta at the last tap, so $K$ collapses to $1$; (ii)
the gate bias is set to a constant, making $\sigma(\cdot)$ a scalar $g$; (iii) the
angle projection is set to
$A/(\tau g)$ where $A = \operatorname{diag}(\omega)W_{\mathrm{out}}W_{\mathrm{in}}$;
(iv) the projection bias is zeroed. The two models then compute the same $\theta$ to
$1.5\times10^{-5}$ in float32, and the constructed $W_\omega$ has rank $2$ of $64$.

\medskip
\noindent\emph{RoPE inside Selective RoPE} is unconditional: $W_\omega := 0$ with
bias $b$ gives $\theta_t = \tau(t{+}1)b$, an exact index clock (constant to
$7\times10^{-9}$).

\medskip
\noindent\emph{RoPE inside MapFormer} costs something, because $\varphi$ has no bias
term: a constant increment requires every embedding row to share its projection onto
$W_{\mathrm{in}}$. Taking $W_{\mathrm{in}} = [I_r\;0]$ and pinning the first $r$
embedding coordinates achieves it exactly, at a price of $r$ of the $d$ embedding
dimensions.

\medskip
\noindent Consequently \textbf{every comparison inside this column asks whether a
constraint is worth keeping, and none of them is about expressivity.}

\subsection{The factorisation identity}

Cumulative summation is linear, so it commutes with the readout:
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:factor}
  \theta_t \;=\; \omega \odot \textstyle\sum_{u \le t} W_{\mathrm{out}}W_{\mathrm{in}}x_u
  \;=\; \operatorname{diag}(\omega)\,W_{\mathrm{out}}
        \textstyle\sum_{u\le t} W_{\mathrm{in}}x_u .
\end{equation}
All $H\nb = 64$ of MapFormer's phase channels are linear readouts of a single
$r$-dimensional accumulator (checked against the implementation at
$1.5\times10^{-5}$ in float32). The multi-scale frequency ladder is a \emph{view} of
one quantity, not $64$ independent clocks --- which is what makes the phase a
coherent position code rather than a bag of unrelated oscillators.

\subsection{Coherence rank}
\label{sec:rank}

Define the \textbf{coherence rank} $\rho$ as the dimension of the affine span of
$\{\theta_t\}$, i.e.\ $\operatorname{rank}[\,A\!\cdot\!B \mid A\varphi(0)\,]$ when
$\varphi(x) = Bx$. The affine part matters: RoPE's constant $\varphi$ has $B=0$, so
the linear rank alone would read $0$. Then $\rho = 1$ for RoPE, $r$ for MapFormer,
and generically $H\nb$ for Selective RoPE.

Two thresholds bracket the useful range. Below, a packing argument: if $N^D$
positions are carried by a $\rho$-dimensional code, the minimum separation of
distinct positions falls at worst like
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:packing}
  \min_{p\neq p'} \lVert \theta_p - \theta_{p'}\rVert \;\lesssim\; N^{-\max(D/\rho,\,1)},
\end{equation}
saturating at the lattice's own spacing once $\rho \ge D$. This is an upper bound
and is not tight: measured exponents track it at $D\le3$ and run steeper at $D=5$
($-2.99$ against a predicted $-2.50$). Above, once $\rho \ge D$ the bound says
nothing further, and what remains is a question about conditioning rather than
capacity --- see $\S$\ref{sec:measured}.

\subsection{What $K>1$ costs}
\label{sec:group}

With $K=1$ and $\varphi$ linear, \eqref{eq:gen} is exactly the action of the free
\emph{commutative} monoid on tokens: the phase depends on the multiset of tokens
traversed and nothing else, so
$\theta(a\ \text{then}\ b) = \theta(b\ \text{then}\ a)$ and
$\prod_{u\le t}R(\omega\Delta_u) = R(\omega\sum_{u\le t}\Delta_u)$. That collapse is
what buys the parallel prefix scan.

A convolution with $K>1$ makes a token's increment depend on its neighbours, so the
phase stops being a function of the multiset and the map from action sequences to
$SO(2)^{\nb}$ is no longer a homomorphism. Commutativity is the central
computational property here, and it is also the stated limitation: a turn/forward
action space, where displacement depends on accumulated heading, is not
representable by a fixed per-token increment.

\section{The magnitude axis}
\label{sec:pope}

The other factor of \eqref{eq:polar}. Three occupants, differing in whether content
enters instantaneously or by accumulation.

\paragraph{PoPE} (Gopalakrishnan et al., 2025) is defined by \emph{deleting} a term.
RoPE's real-valued $q$ carries an implicit intrinsic phase $\psi^q$, and
\eqref{eq:polar} shows it entering the cosine alongside the positional angle ---
what PoPE calls an entanglement of ``what'' with ``where''. PoPE removes the
interaction $\psi^k - \psi^q$ and keeps only a content-dependent \emph{magnitude},
$\mu = \softplus(\cdot) \ge 0$, with the phase a pure index clock plus a learned
per-(head, frequency) constant $\delta_c$. It is the exact complement of MapFormer,
which amplifies the same phase term into a learned accumulator.

\paragraph{Forget gates.} FoX computes a scalar $f_t = \sigma(w_f^\top x_t + b_f)$
per head and adds $\sum_{j<\ell\le t}\log f_\ell$ to the logit. Gated linear RNNs
(GLA, Mamba, Mamba-2) do the same thing inside a recurrence,
$S_t = \operatorname{Diag}(\alpha_t)S_{t-1} + k_tv_t$. GRAPE proves FoX is an exact
instance of its additive family. All are accumulated content-dependent magnitude,
with $\log\gamma < 0$ by construction.

\paragraph{ALiBi} is the index-driven, content-free member: a linear-in-lag penalty,
realisable by a defective generator.

\section{One equation}
\label{sec:unify}

The two axes are disjoint coordinates of a single object. Put each token's
contribution in \emph{log}-polar form. Let $c^q, c^k, G : \R^d \to \C^{\nb}$ and set
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:unified}
  \boxed{\;
  \tilde q_t = \exp\!\big(c^q(x_t) - \overline{S_t}\big),\qquad
  \tilde k_s = \exp\!\big(c^k(x_s) + S_s\big),\qquad
  S_t = \textstyle\sum_{u\le t} G(x_u),}
\end{equation}
componentwise, with $a_{ts} = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt{\hd}}\sum_c
\operatorname{Re}[\overline{\tilde q_t^{(c)}}\,\tilde k_s^{(c)}]$. Writing
$c = \gamma + i\pi$ and $G = \delta + i\eta$ with all four real,
\begin{equation}
  a_{ts} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\hd}}\sum_c
  \underbrace{e^{\,\gamma^q_{t,c}+\gamma^k_{s,c}+\sum_{t<u\le s}\delta_{u,c}}}_{\text{magnitude}}
  \cos\Big(\underbrace{\pi^k_{s,c}-\pi^q_{t,c}+\textstyle\sum_{t<u\le s}\eta_{u,c}}_{\text{phase}}\Big).
\end{equation}

\noindent $c$ is the instantaneous complex log and $G$ the accumulated one; real
parts are magnitude, imaginary parts are phase. Every mechanism in
$\S\S$\ref{sec:phase}--\ref{sec:pope} is a choice of which of the four real fields
$\gamma, \pi, \delta, \eta$ is non-trivial.

\paragraph{The conjugate is load-bearing, and the two accumulators carry opposite
signs.} The Hermitian form $\overline{\tilde q}\,\tilde k$ \emph{subtracts} phases
and \emph{adds} log-magnitudes. So $\operatorname{Im}G$ must be carried with the
\emph{same} sign on $q$ and $k$ to yield $\sum_{t<u\le s}\eta_u$, while
$\operatorname{Re}G$ must be carried \emph{oppositely} to yield
$\sum_{t<u\le s}\delta_u$; carried with the same sign it would give
$\sum_{u\le t} + \sum_{u\le s}$, a joint scale carrying no information about
separation. Perturbing the prefix $u\le t$ and asking whether the score moves --- a
relative score must not:

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{llr}
\toprule
$\operatorname{Re}G$ & $\operatorname{Im}G$ & relative prefix leak \\
\midrule
\textbf{opposed} & \textbf{aligned} & $\mathbf{3.4\times10^{-16}}$ \\
opposed & opposed & $1.4$ \\
aligned & aligned & $3.1$ \\
aligned & opposed & $7.1$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent Exactly one of the four combinations is interval-relative, and it is the
one \eqref{eq:unified} writes. With $\delta = \log\gamma < 0$ that slot \emph{is} the
forget gate; with $\eta$ content-dependent the phase slot is MapFormer and Selective
RoPE. \textbf{MapPoPE is not a third mechanism} --- PoPE constrains $c$, the phase
family constrains $\operatorname{Im}G$, the coordinates are disjoint, so imposing
both is an immediate consequence rather than a design.

\paragraph{Interval-invariance is the right notion of ``relative''.} With
content-dependent increments the logit is not a function of $s-t$ and the score
matrix is not Toeplitz. The property that survives, and the one that matters, is
that the score depends on the tokens in $(t,s]$ and not on the prefix.

\section{The relational map}
\label{sec:map}

Eight axes separate everything in the arena. They are not independent, but no two
mechanisms agree on all eight.

\begin{enumerate}\itemsep1pt
\item[\textbf{A1}] \textbf{Factor} --- magnitude ($\operatorname{Re}$), phase
  ($\operatorname{Im}$), both, or neither.
\item[\textbf{A2}] \textbf{Locus} --- instantaneous ($c$) or accumulated ($G$).
\item[\textbf{A3}] \textbf{Driver} --- the index, the token content, a known
  position, or a query--key \emph{pair}.
\item[\textbf{A4}] \textbf{Algebra} --- semisimple diagonal ($\C^\times$), unipotent,
  non-semisimple, or non-abelian.
\item[\textbf{A5}] \textbf{Sign} --- may the increment be negative?
\item[\textbf{A6}] \textbf{Rank} of the content-to-increment map.
\item[\textbf{A7}] \textbf{Host} --- softmax attention, or linear attention / SSM.
\item[\textbf{A8}] \textbf{Composition} --- parallel prefix sum, path-ordered
  product, or neither.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Reading the map}

\emph{(i) The frame's own cells are full.} Crossing A1 (magnitude / phase) with A3
(index / content) gives four cells and all four are occupied: RoPE, ALiBi, Mamba-3's
rotation, FoX. GRAPE holds the index-multiplicative and content-additive cells;
Mamba-3, Selective RoPE and CARoPE hold content-phase. The remaining structure is
\textbf{content-dependent, signed, orthogonal and still abelian} --- contextual
multiplicative --- which is exactly where MapFormer, Selective RoPE and CARoPE sit.

\emph{(ii) A5 is settled next door and unapplied here.} Sarrof et al.\ (2024)
observed that SSM gates are ``always nonnegative \dots\ due to exponential or sigmoid
parameterizations'', with a theorem: such models cannot recognise \textsc{parity}.
Grazzi et al.\ (ICLR 2025) proved the eigenvalue version and fixed it by extending
the range to $[-1,1]$; PaTH adopts it deliberately; RWKV-7 restricts it only for
training stability; and Selective RoPE's $\S$4.2 already demonstrates single-layer
Transformer parity from rotations that ``model \emph{flips}''. Yet CARoPE, GRAPE-AP
and CoPE are all monotone --- by softplus, by construction, by sigmoid --- and none
cites any of that literature. $\S$\ref{sec:sign} measures what the constraint costs
where it can be seen.

\emph{(iii) A6 is thinner than a blank column suggests.} Two rows constrain something
rank-like for reasons of their own: PaTH's Householder factors are $I + $rank-$1$ by
definition, and CARoPE emits one scalar per head, putting its content-to-angle map
\emph{below} MapFormer's $r$ rather than above it. The nearest deliberate study is
LieRE's generator tile-size sweep, which varies density on the \emph{output} side and
peaks in the interior at $8\times8$. What no one sweeps is the \emph{input-side}
bottleneck: how many dimensions of content may steer the angle.

\emph{(iv) A7 is nearly free.} Selective RoPE runs in both hosts and Mamba-3 crosses
over by state-space duality, so ``softmax versus SSM'' separates implementations
rather than mechanisms.

\section{The memory reading: what the frame looks like from the other side}
\label{sec:memory}

A large literature --- fast weight programmers, linear attention, gated linear
RNNs, state-space models and their hybrids --- studies the same equation while
asking a different question. It is worth stating the correspondence, because the
two literatures partition the design space almost perfectly and neither says so.

\paragraph{One primitive, two forms.} The outer product $v_u \otimes k_u$ binds a
value to a key, and superposing bindings gives an associative memory read by a
query. The object appears as Kohonen's correlation-matrix memory (1972),
Schmidhuber's fast weight programmer (1992), the recurrent form of linear attention
(Katharopoulos et al., 2020), the hidden state of a scalar-$A$ state-space model,
Smolensky's tensor-product role--filler binding (1990), and --- via the dual form of
Irie et al.\ (2022) --- the weight matrix of any linearly-parameterised layer trained
by gradient descent. Schlag et al.\ (2021) made the linear-attention correspondence
explicit. This gives every mechanism two readings:
\[
  \underbrace{W_t = \textstyle\sum_{u\le t} A(t-u)\,v_u k_u^{\!\top},\quad y_t = W_tq_t}_{\text{\emph{primal}: compress, bounded state}}
  \qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad
  \underbrace{a_{ts} = \tilde q_t\!\cdot\!\tilde k_s \text{ over kept items}}_{\text{\emph{dual}: keep and attend}}
\]

\paragraph{The positional operator is the memory's transport kernel.} $A(d)$ is what
happens to a stored trace as it ages $d$ steps, and $\S$\ref{sec:classification}
says there are two things it can do:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
slot & positional reading & memory reading \\
\midrule
$\operatorname{Re}G$ & magnitude / decay & \textbf{how fast the trace is forgotten} \\
$\operatorname{Im}G$ & phase / rotation  & \textbf{how the trace is re-addressed as it ages} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent It also identifies A8 with the primal/dual choice: \textbf{a positional
mechanism admits a parallel prefix scan exactly when the memory admits a bounded
recurrent state}, because both are the group law $A(d)A(e) = A(d+e)$. That is one
condition, not two.

\paragraph{The two literatures have divided the slots without noticing.} The
fast-weight literature owns $\operatorname{Re}G$ exhaustively. Irie and Gershman's
review (TMLR 2025) categorises the whole modern family by \emph{decay type} ---
RetNet a constant scalar, Mamba-2 a scalar, GLA a rate per row of the fast weight
--- and the words ``rotary'', ``RoPE'' and ``positional'' do not occur in it. The
positional-encoding literature owns $\operatorname{Im}G$ and treats decay as a
side-condition. Mamba-3 is the first design to claim both deliberately, and it
arrives from the memory side, stating the crossing as a proposition on
``data-dependent RoPE equivalence''. The hybrid quadratic-linear transformers of
Irie et al.\ (2025) use RoPE, but only as an off-the-shelf component inside the
attention half rather than as a design axis.

\paragraph{The non-abelian family is the rewritable-memory family.} The first row of
the out-of-frame table is not a collection of exceptions. DeltaNet, PaTH and RWKV-7
replace the \emph{additive} Hebbian update with a \emph{delta rule},
\[
  W_t = W_{t-1} + \sigma(\beta_t)\big(v_t - W_{t-1}\phi(k_t)\big)\otimes\phi(k_t),
\]
whose state-transition matrix $I - \sigma(\beta_t)\phi\phi^{\!\top}$ is a
Householder-like reflection rather than a scalar. Memory becomes rewritable ---
stale associations are corrected instead of accumulated --- and the price is exactly
the group law: reflections do not commute, so no $A(d)$ and no $S_t$ exist and the
composition is a path-ordered product. \textbf{What that family trades away is the
positional structure, and what it buys is a memory that can overwrite.} It is also
where the sign axis of $\S$\ref{sec:map}(ii) is deliberately exercised: with
$\sigma = 2\,\mathrm{sigmoid}$ the eigenvalues lie in $(-1,1)$ \emph{including
negative values}, which is what unlocks parity and modular state tracking.

\paragraph{And MapEM's lift is a binding.} The tensor product
$q^x \otimes q^p$ of $\S$\ref{sec:emtem} is Smolensky's role--filler binding: content
as filler, position as role. Read from the memory side, MapEM stores each
observation bound to its place rather than superposing the two channels additively
--- which is the same trade the primal/dual axis makes, one level down.

\medskip
\noindent Everything in this section is a change of reading, not of mechanism. It
matters because it says where to look for the unoccupied designs: the cells that are
empty in one literature are usually the ones the other has already filled.

\section{Where MapEM and TEM-t sit}
\label{sec:emtem}

Two mechanisms from the cognitive-map literature are placeable, and the placements
are opposite.

\paragraph{MapEM lifts the frame rather than leaving it.} MapEM computes two
attention matrices and multiplies them elementwise,
$A = \softmax(A_X \odot A_P)$. With $(A_X)_{ts} = q^x_t\!\cdot\!k^x_s$ and
$(A_P)_{ts} = q^p_t\!\cdot\!k^p_s$,
\[
  (A_X \odot A_P)_{ts} \;=\; \big(q^x_t \otimes q^p_t\big)\cdot\big(k^x_s \otimes k^p_s\big),
\]
so the Hadamard product \emph{is} a single bilinear form --- on the tensor product of
the content and position spaces, of dimension $d_xd_p$ rather than $d$ (verified to
$1.9\times10^{-6}$ in float32). Everything in $\S$\ref{sec:unify} applies with $c$ and
$G$ valued in $\C^{d_xd_p}$.

That is sharper than ``an AND-gate rather than an OR-gate'', and it says what the lift
costs: a rank-one tensor $q^x\otimes q^p$ can express content-\emph{and}-position but
not content-\emph{or}-position, and there is no fallback channel when either factor is
uninformative. It predicts the observed sensitivity to how the position channel is
initialised --- a single shared $p_0$ beats separate $q^p_0, k^p_0$ by $+0.358$ on a
matching task ($3/3$ seeds) --- because with no additive fallback a mis-set position
factor multiplies the whole score instead of being outvoted.

\paragraph{TEM-t is the same machine with two conditions removed.} TEM-t
(Whittington et al., 2022) generates position recurrently,
$e_t = \sigma(e_{t-1}W_{a_t})$ with $\sigma$ a ReLU and $W_a$ a general per-action
matrix, then sets $Q = K = W_e e$ and $V = W_x x$; content is absent from the logit
entirely and enters only through the values.

Its logit is $e_t^\top W_e^\top W_e\,e_s$. Writing $e_s = W_{t\to s}e_t$ and
$e_t = W_{0\to t}e_0$, with $W_e$ orthogonal this is
$e_0^\top W_{0\to t}^{\top}W_{t\to s}W_{0\to t}e_0$ --- \textbf{the interval operator
conjugated by the prefix}. Conjugation is trivial exactly when the group is abelian:

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lc}
\toprule
transition group & logit depends on the interval alone? \\
\midrule
abelian (block rotations), no $\sigma$ & \textbf{yes} \\
general $SO(d)$, no $\sigma$           & no \\
abelian, with ReLU                     & no \\
general $SO(d)$, with ReLU             & no \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent \textbf{MapFormer is precisely the surviving row}: $SO(2)^{\nb}$ is abelian
by construction and there is no nonlinearity on the path. TEM-t breaks both, and
either break alone suffices.

This is the mechanical account of a large empirical cost. No group law means no
$S_t$, hence no parallel prefix scan, hence a sequential loop: at $L=2048$ a faithful
TEM is $120\times$ slower than at $L=128$ and $1632\times$ slower in absolute terms
than a MapFormer of $20\times$ \emph{more} parameters. The constant includes
interpreter overhead; the shape is the missing group law. TEM-t is 2022 and MapFormer
2025, so on this axis the later contribution is the \emph{restriction} --- abelian and
linear --- that buys the scan and the relative law.

\section{What is measured}
\label{sec:measured}

The axes above are separable only on a task where position must be reconstructed from
the input rather than read off the index. All measurements below use a torus
grid-navigation task: an agent takes actions on an $N\times N$ torus, observations are
aliased across cells, and the model predicts the observation at revisited cells.
Standard errors are over seeds; \textsc{mde} is $2.8\,\mathrm{sd}/\sqrt{n}$, and a
contrast inside its \textsc{mde} is reported as unmeasured.

\subsection{The sign of the increment}
\label{sec:sign}

Six arms $\times$ $12$ seeds, one batch, $r=4$ throughout, \textbf{parameter count
identical at $204{,}757$} --- the constraint adds nothing and does not touch the rank.

\toprule
arm & increment & $T{=}128$ & $T{=}512$ & $T{=}1024$ & final loss \\
\midrule
signed  & $W_{\mathrm{out}}W_{\mathrm{in}}x$ & $1.000$ & $0.978$ & $0.922$ & $0.0002$ \\
\midrule
$|\cdot|$ & $|W_{\mathrm{out}}W_{\mathrm{in}}x|$ & $0.946$ & $0.675$ & $0.558$ & $0.171$ \\
softplus  & GRAPE-AP form                       & $0.977$ & $0.798$ & $0.584$ & $0.068$ \\
CARoPE    & $1/(\softplus(\cdot)+1)$            & $0.900$ & $0.809$ & $0.645$ & $0.293$ \\

\noindent\textbf{At matched training loss, signed path integration beats an index code
by $+0.123$ and $+0.195$ at $T=512$ and $T=1024$ ($12/12$ seeds); monotone path
integration beats an index code nowhere} --- $-0.028$ at $T=128$ and unmeasured
beyond. The entire measured value of a content-dependent phase over a plain index
clock, on this task, requires the increment to be signed.

The learned code shows why. The opposition score
$\lVert\Delta(+x)+\Delta(-x)\rVert / \overline{\lVert\Delta\rVert}$ is $0$ when
opposite actions cancel exactly and $2$ when they are identical:

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\toprule
arm & oppose$_x$ & oppose$_y$ & $|\cos(N,E)|$ \\
\midrule
signed    & $\mathbf{0.125}$ & $\mathbf{0.106}$ & $0.218$ \\
$|\cdot|$ & $1.849$ & $1.855$ & $0.587$ \\
softplus  & $1.905$ & $1.885$ & $0.723$ \\
CARoPE    & $\mathbf{1.981}$ & $\mathbf{1.977}$ & $\mathbf{0.930}$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent CARoPE's published parameterisation reaches $1.98$ of a possible $2.00$,
with its north and east axes all but collapsed onto one another. These models do not
merely score worse --- they cannot represent a $-1$ action, and the probe confirms
they do not.

\emph{Limitation.} At the training length the signed baseline is at $1.000\pm0.000$,
so accuracy there cannot show a deficit of any size. The training-length effect is
visible in the \emph{loss} instead: every constrained arm fits strictly worse on
$12/12$ seeds at identical parameters.

\emph{Scope.} This is a replication in a new regime. The constraint and its
consequence for parity are established (\S\ref{sec:map}(ii)); what is new is
navigation rather than formal languages, and an isolation in which $|\Delta|$ against
$\Delta$ is a single operation at identical parameter count.

\subsection{Rank}

The bottleneck $r$ is meant to be the dimensionality of the action space --- for a
$2$D grid, a $2$-vector --- which makes $r=2$ sufficient \emph{by construction}. It is
not sufficient in practice. Against $r=2$ at $8\times$ the training length ($T=1024$
against a training length of $128$; $8$ seeds):

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\toprule
 & params added & $T{=}512$ & $T{=}1024$ \\
\midrule
$r=4$  & $+384$    & $+0.038$ & $\mathbf{+0.085}$ ($8/8$, $t\,3.57$) \\
$r=8$  & $+1{,}152$ & $+0.037$ & $+0.091$ \\
$r=16$ & $+2{,}688$ & $+0.028$ & $+0.079$ \\
$r=32$ & $+5{,}760$ & $+0.038$ & $+0.095$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent This is a \textbf{step at $r=2$, not a capacity slope}: flat from $r=4$ to
$r=32$. The cause is conditioning, not capacity. Given four dimensions the model still
places its actions in a $2$-plane ($100.0\%$ of spectral energy; $99.96\%$ even at
$r=32$), vindicating the dimensional argument --- what fails is the \emph{basis} that
$r=2$ is forced into:

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\toprule
 & opposition ($N{+}S\approx0$) & $|\cos(N,E)|$ & obs/action norm \\
\midrule
$r=2$ & $0.495$ & $0.783$ & $0.139$ \\
$r=4$ & $\mathbf{0.092}$ & $\mathbf{0.175}$ & $0.042$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent At $r=2$ opposite actions fail to cancel by half the action scale and north
and east are nearly parallel. The seed standard deviation also falls from $0.064$ to
$0.012$ at $T=1024$, which matters because it sets every detectable effect size.

Two further observations. The deficit does \emph{not} grow with the dimensionality of
the world: at $D=5$, $r=2$ is unimpaired ($0.896$, its best of three conditions), so
the packing account of \eqref{eq:packing} does not explain it. And an unconstrained
generator cannot be projected below rank $\approx16$ post hoc while $r=4$ trains fine
--- trained-at-rank and truncated-to-rank are different objects.

\subsection{What the other constraints are worth}

Selective RoPE's three additions to MapFormer's generator, measured on the torus at
$T=1024$ and on a parity task at $L=128$; increments against MapFormer at $r=2$.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lrll}
\toprule
constraint removed & params & parity $L{=}128$ & torus $T{=}1024$ \\
\midrule
$K=1 \to 4$ (causal conv)      & $+193$     & $-0.020$ & $-0.064$ ($t\,{-}1.99$) \\
$m = r \to H\nb$ (full rank)   & $+7{,}873$ & $-0.020$ & $+0.058$ ($7/8$) \\

\midrule
\emph{$r=2\to4$, constraint kept} & $\mathbf{+384}$ & --- & $\mathbf{+0.085}$ ($8/8$) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent Two readings. First, \textbf{the two $\approx$8k-parameter knobs are
statistically indistinguishable from each other} ($+0.018$ and $+0.028$, both inside
their \textsc{mde}s), so what they buy is most plausibly capacity rather than either
named mechanism --- and widening the bottleneck MapFormer already has buys the same
thing for $384$ parameters, $21\times$ cheaper. Second, the convolution is the only
knob negative on both tasks, consistent with $\S$\ref{sec:group}: $K>1$ costs the
homomorphism.

\emph{Caveat.} These arms replace the readout $\operatorname{diag}(\omega)W_{\mathrm{out}}$
by $\tau W_2$. For the conv and gate rows the function class is preserved exactly ---
it is the construction of $\S$\ref{sec:incl} --- so the difference is initialisation
(a geometric frequency ladder against a default draw) plus one scalar; those effects
are attributable up to initialisation. The two full-rank rows also add weight
normalisation and a bias, so their function class genuinely differs.

A separate probe rules out the natural mechanism for the gate. If the sigmoid worked
by suppressing observation tokens --- which MapFormer must instead learn to send to
$\Delta\approx0$ --- the gate would be near zero on observations. It is $0.416$, and
the action/observation ratio is $1.35\times$ on the torus where the gate helps and
$1.54\times$ on parity where it hurts.

\subsection{The navigation regime}

Every paper in $\S$\ref{sec:map} evaluates on language, recall, state tracking, or ---
for LieRE and Algebraic PE --- images, video and trees. None runs grid navigation, and
it is the regime in which these axes are distinguishable at all.

The basic separation is large. On a task where the model explores with observations
revealed and then continues blind, predicting the observation at each cell, path
integration scores $0.730 \pm 0.247$ ($n=5$, $64^2$) against $0.154 \pm 0.018$ for an
index code, chance $0.0625$, with no seed overlap. The axis is path integration and not
the encoding: PoPE with path integration scores $0.847$ against $0.117$ for PoPE with
an index. On the standard revisit task the position effect is $+0.461$ against $+0.003$
for the encoding --- a factor of $150$.

Two boundaries are worth stating. The effect is not a property of navigation as such
but of \emph{map extent}: at matched observation aliasing it is $-0.010$, $+0.015$ and
$+0.305$ for $32$, $128$ and $512$ occupied cells --- a threshold, not a gradient. And
it does not survive rotation-based action spaces: under turn/turn/forward, where
displacement depends on accumulated heading, the effect falls from $+0.438$ to $+0.050$,
which is $\S$\ref{sec:group}'s commutativity limitation made concrete. Recording the
absolute displacement instead of the commanded turn restores it to $+0.488$.

\section{Open questions}

\begin{itemize}\itemsep3pt
\item \textbf{The input-side rank.} $r=2$ is the worst setting and $r=4$ fixes it for
  $384$ parameters, but nothing explains why the optimiser finds a skewed basis at
  $r=2$ and a well-conditioned one at $r=4$ when the learned solution occupies a
  $2$-plane either way.
\item \textbf{The OOD-length axis.} Rank, forget gating, PoPE and Kalman-style
  correction all help at extrapolated length and only there. Four mechanisms, one
  axis, no account.
\item \textbf{Defective generators.} Jordan-RoPE occupies the cell; what polynomial
  factors in the lag are \emph{for} remains open, and no navigation evaluation exists.
\item \textbf{Sign on language.} A monotone clock cannot represent a $-1$ action, and
  on navigation that costs everything. On text HGRN's own ablation finds a
  data-dependent phase worse than a constant one. Whether the sign matters there, and
  for what, is untested.
\item \textbf{Non-commuting action spaces.} Rotation-based actions defeat a fixed
  per-token increment. Allocentric recoding sidesteps it wherever heading is known;
  the non-abelian mechanisms (PaTH, DeltaNet, RWKV-7) address it directly and have
  never been evaluated on navigation.
\end{itemize}
